🐇 WR ERP — Weekly Inventory

Step-by-step, click-by-click · physical count that becomes your stock

Before you start — Log in

Open the app and sign in with your work email and password. The top bar should read LIVE 🔒 before you post anything.

What this covers

The Weekly Inventory count — your recurring physical stock-take. You count what’s on the shelf, the app compares it to the book stock, and on approval your count becomes the stock while gaps are logged as variances. (Setting day-1 opening stock in bulk is an Admin task — see the Operating Workflow & Role Playbooks guide → Admin functions.)

Weekly Inventory — direct encoding  ·  Operations → Weekly Inventory  ·  posted by the Warehouse/Inventory Manager
1

Open Weekly Inventory & start a new count

Sidebar → OPERATIONS → 🧾 Weekly Inventory (address …/#/count_sessions). You see the list of past counts. Click the green New button.

2

Set location, date & scope, then Load items

The sheet opens in draft. Set Location (bulacan/pasig), Date, and Scope (All, or just Ingredients / Supplies / Stickers / Sellable Products). Click ⬇ Load items — the app fills the Book qty automatically.

NOTE
Book qty is auto-generated and locked. It’s the system’s expected stock; you only type what you physically counted.
3

Encode the counted quantities

Rows are grouped Category → base material, with each item’s Raw and Processed forms together and Kitchen Supplies in their own group. Every row has a #. Type the count into COUNTED; the VARIANCE shows the gap and turns red when it’s not zero.

SHORTCUT
＝ Fill counted with book qty pre-fills every count with the book number, then you only fix the rows that differ.
THE # MATCHES YOUR PAPER
The on-screen # is the same number on the printed PDF and the Excel — so “row 5 = 45” lands on the exact row here.
4

Print or export for the floor

From the bottom bar: 🖨 Print count sheet makes a PDF (fixed header, Page X of Y, item #); ⬇ Excel template makes a styled sheet with a blank Counted column. Both match the screen order exactly. If your team filled the Excel offline, ⬆ Upload counts reads it back and fills Counted by SKU.

5

Save draft, then Approve & post

💾 Save draft keeps progress. When done and checked, the Warehouse / Inventory Manager clicks ✔ Approve & post.

WHO POSTS — AND WHAT IT DOES
Posting is the Warehouse / Inventory Manager’s job. It writes the counted quantity as the new stock, and each gap (counted − book) is logged as a shrinkage variance for review.
✓

How to know it worked

The status pill switches to approved and a green “Approved — posted to stock” badge appears; the sheet goes read-only.

CONFIRMED WHEN YOU SEE
① the approved pill, ② the green Approved — posted to stock badge, ③ new numbers in Live Stock, ④ any gaps waiting in Variances (reviewed by Admin/Owner).
